Animal Welfare

Wildlife Welfare Rules

The welfare of birds and other wildlife takes priority over visitor entertainment or convenience. Unless specifically authorized by BioWild staff, visitors must not touch, feed, chase, capture, frighten, call, handle, release or otherwise interfere with animals.

  • Do not cross animal-care barriers or enter restricted habitats.
  • Do not feed wildlife without express authorization.
  • Do not use flash, speakers, playback calls, lasers, drones or other equipment in a way that disturbs wildlife.
  • Do not remove feathers, eggs, plants, nests, natural materials or sanctuary property.
  • Do not bring an animal into the sanctuary without prior authorization, except where applicable law requires accommodation.
  • Do not release domestic, captive or rescued wildlife on BioWild property without authorization.
Wildlife is not guaranteed to be visible. Because animal welfare, rehabilitation needs, weather and natural behavior can change, BioWild cannot guarantee that a particular bird or species will be visible during a visit.
