Privacy at a glance

What BioWild means by responsible data use.

BioWild is a bird-sanctuary and conservation project, so information may come from ordinary customer interactions as well as wildlife observations, uploaded media, school programs and conservation technology. We try to keep those uses clear and proportionate.

01

What we collect

Contact details, booking or order information, voluntary program details, uploaded bird media, sightings, technical data and communications when relevant to a BioWild service.

02

Why we use it

To operate visits, answer requests, manage programs, provide digital tools, support conservation records, improve services, protect security and meet legal obligations.

03

When it is shared

Only where reasonably necessary with authorized service providers, competent authorities, program partners or other recipients described in this Policy and permitted by law.

04

Your control

You may have rights to access, correct, delete/cancel, oppose certain uses, obtain portability, revoke consent where applicable and raise concerns with the competent authority.

        This Privacy Policy applies to personal information handled through the BioWild website and BioWild sanctuary-related activities, including visits, tickets, contact requests, merchandise, sponsorships, memberships, volunteering, educational programs, Birdify, bird sightings, events and other services that link to or reference this Policy.

        In this Policy, “BioWild,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to the operator of the BioWild project and its relevant services. Before public commercial launch, the full legal name and legally required operator information should replace any project-stage description.

        BioWild intends to handle personal data consistently with applicable Panamanian law, including Law 81 of March 26, 2019 on Personal Data Protection and Executive Decree 285 of May 28, 2021, which regulates that Law.

        These rules establish principles, rights, obligations and procedures concerning personal-data processing in Panama. Where another mandatory law or specific regulatory rule applies to a particular activity, that requirement also remains applicable.

        Official authority The National Authority for Transparency and Access to Information (ANTAI) is Panama's data-protection authority for the general regime under Law 81.

        Depending on the service, BioWild may process information such as:

        • name, email address, telephone number and communication details;
        • ticket, reservation, event, visitor-category and group information;
        • order, merchandise, sponsorship, membership or donation-request information;
        • volunteer applications, school-program registrations and program-related information;
        • messages, questions, feedback, support requests and newsletter preferences;
        • photos, audio, bird observations, dates and locations voluntarily submitted to Birdify or sighting features;
        • technical data such as device/browser information, basic logs, security events and locally stored preferences where used;
        • photographs or recordings created during BioWild activities where there is an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

        BioWild should not request sensitive information merely because a form can collect it. Collection should be proportionate to the service and limited to what is reasonably necessary.

        BioWild may use personal information to provide a requested service, communicate with you, manage capacity and safety, process or prepare orders, administer sponsorships or programs, maintain conservation records, operate Birdify, prevent misuse, protect systems, improve services, comply with lawful requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

        Where Panamanian law requires consent, BioWild should seek prior, informed and unequivocal consent. In other situations, processing may be permitted because another legal basis or statutory exception applies.

        Purpose limitation Information collected for one purpose should not be quietly repurposed for an incompatible use without an appropriate legal basis and, where required, new notice or consent.

        Birdify may allow a user to upload a photograph, audio recording or other observation material so BioWild can provide a bird-identification or educational result. Those files may contain personal information, voices, background images, embedded metadata or location information.

        • Upload only material you are authorized to share.
        • Avoid including unrelated people, private conversations or sensitive information.
        • Where technically feasible, BioWild should minimize unnecessary metadata and restrict internal access.
        • Automated identification outputs may be stored with the submission if needed to deliver, troubleshoot or improve the feature.

        If BioWild later uses uploaded media to train or evaluate machine-learning models beyond providing the user-requested service, that use should be clearly disclosed and supported by an appropriate legal basis before it occurs.

        A sighting record may contain a species, date, time, photograph, audio recording, observer comments and geographic location. If a submission is linked to your account or contact details, it can also constitute personal information.

        Exact wildlife locations can create conservation risks, especially for nests, threatened species, rehabilitation areas or locations vulnerable to disturbance, capture or trafficking. BioWild may therefore generalize, delay, restrict or withhold precise wildlife coordinates from public display while retaining a more precise record for authorized conservation purposes where lawful.

        Conservation privacy is different from personal privacy. Protecting the location of an animal is not the same legal concept as protecting a person's data, but BioWild may need to do both at the same time.

        BioWild may process contact, transaction-request, reservation, delivery/pickup and sponsorship information to organize the transaction and communicate with the customer or sponsor.

        If BioWild connects a third-party payment processor, that provider may collect payment-card or financial information directly under its own privacy notice and security controls. Unless BioWild expressly states otherwise after a secure integration is implemented, users should not assume that BioWild itself stores full payment-card details.

        Sponsorships are symbolic. Personal information used for certificates, recognition, updates or sponsorship communications does not create ownership or custody rights over a bird.

        Volunteer, school, research, workshop or youth programs may require additional information about eligibility, availability, emergency contacts, parental/guardian authorization, accessibility needs or safety requirements. BioWild should request only information appropriate to the program.

        Children and teenagers deserve additional privacy care. Where consent from a parent, guardian or another authorized adult is legally required, BioWild should obtain it before processing the relevant data. Public posting of identifiable images or names of minors should be handled cautiously and with appropriate authorization.

        The BioWild website may use cookies, local storage or similar browser technologies for functions such as keeping interface preferences, remembering a cart, security, analytics or other website operations.

        Essential technologies may be necessary for requested website functions. Optional analytics, personalization or marketing technologies should be described clearly and, where consent is legally required, should not be activated before the appropriate choice is obtained.

        The preference panel on this page stores the choices it records in your browser's local storage. This Privacy Policy code does not itself load third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

        BioWild may disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

        • hosting, email, reservation, payment, communications, security or technical providers acting for a BioWild service;
        • schools, research or conservation partners involved in a program you join, where an appropriate legal basis exists;
        • competent wildlife, veterinary, emergency, judicial, administrative or other authorities where disclosure is legally required or permitted;
        • professional advisers or insurers where reasonably necessary and lawful;
        • a successor organization if BioWild's legal operator or relevant assets are reorganized, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

        BioWild does not intend to sell personal information as a commercial data product. If its practices materially change, this Policy and any required consent mechanism should be updated first.

        Some technology providers may store or process information outside Panama. When BioWild uses a cross-border provider, it should assess the transfer under applicable Panamanian requirements and use appropriate contractual, security or other safeguards where required.

        The mere use of a global cloud, email, map or payment service can create international data flows, so provider selection and configuration should be part of BioWild's privacy review.

        BioWild should retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate recordkeeping, dispute handling, conservation documentation, security, accounting or another applicable legal obligation.

        Different records can require different retention periods. A short-lived contact inquiry may not need to be retained as long as a transaction record or a conservation observation that forms part of a legitimate scientific dataset.

        Deletion is not always immediate. Backups, fraud/security logs, accounting requirements, legal holds or valid scientific records may justify limited continued retention even after an operational record is removed from active use.

        BioWild should use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the information and service, such as access controls, secure authentication, least-privilege access, software updates, backups, confidentiality obligations and secure provider selection.

        No website, network or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their devices, email accounts and passwords and should not send unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary email.

        BioWild may photograph or record activities for conservation documentation, education, internal records or communications. When a person is identifiable, BioWild should rely on an appropriate legal basis and seek consent where required.

        Organized promotional photography should be communicated clearly. Extra care should be taken with children, private program participants, veterinary/rescue areas and anyone who reasonably indicates they do not wish to participate in an optional recording.

        BioWild may link to maps, social networks, payment processors, booking platforms, educational sources or other websites that BioWild does not control. Those providers may collect information under their own privacy policies.

        This Policy does not replace the privacy notice of an independent third-party service. Users should review that provider's terms before submitting information to it.

        Privacy questions and requests may be sent to biowild2026@outlook.com or through the BioWild contact page. BioWild may need to verify the requester's identity in a proportionate manner before disclosing, correcting or deleting personal information.

        If you believe your data-protection rights have been violated, you may also have the right to raise the matter with Panama's competent data-protection authority, ANTAI, subject to its procedures and jurisdiction.

        BioWild may update this Policy to reflect changes in law, services, providers, technology or conservation operations. The effective date should be updated when a material revision is published.

        Before final publication A qualified Panamanian professional should review the final policy against BioWild's actual databases, forms, hosting, analytics, payment providers, retention schedule and legal operator details. A privacy notice must describe what the organization really does, not only what its template says.
