        Your privacy rights

        Rights belong to people, not platforms.

        Panama's data-protection framework recognizes several rights concerning personal information, subject to the legal conditions and exceptions that apply.

        Right 01

        Access

        Ask whether relevant personal information is being processed and obtain information about it where applicable.

        Right 02

        Rectification

        Request correction of inaccurate, outdated or incomplete personal information where appropriate.

        Right 03

        Cancellation

        Request deletion or cancellation when the legal conditions for that right are satisfied.

        Right 04

        Opposition

        Object to certain processing where the applicable legal framework gives you that right.

        Right 05

        Portability

        Request applicable personal information in a usable form under the conditions established by law.

        This Privacy Policy applies to personal information handled through BioWild's website and sanctuary-related activities, including visits, ticket requests, contact forms, merchandise, sponsorships, memberships, volunteering, educational programs, Birdify, bird sightings, events and other services that link to this Policy.

        “BioWild,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to the operator of the BioWild project and its relevant services. Before public commercial launch, legally required operator information should be published accurately.

        BioWild intends to handle personal information consistently with applicable Panamanian law, including Law 81 of March 26, 2019 on Personal Data Protection and Executive Decree 285 of May 28, 2021, which regulates that Law.

        Where another mandatory rule applies to a particular BioWild activity, that requirement also remains applicable.

        Depending on the service, BioWild may handle names, email addresses, phone numbers, reservation details, order or pickup information, sponsorship or membership details, program applications, emergency contacts, communications, uploaded photos or audio, bird observations, location information, device permissions, browser information and basic technical or security records.

        Data minimization matters. BioWild should avoid requesting sensitive or unrelated information unless it is reasonably necessary, lawful and protected appropriately.

        Information may come directly from you, from browser or device functions you choose to use, from a parent, guardian, school or organization involved in an authorized program, or from a lawful provider or partner relevant to a BioWild service.

        Where information comes from another source and applicable law requires notice, BioWild should take appropriate steps to provide it.

        BioWild may use personal information to answer inquiries, manage visits and reservations, coordinate Shop orders, provide sponsorship or membership benefits, operate volunteering and educational programs, provide Birdify and bird-sighting tools, maintain security, communicate service updates and meet legitimate legal or administrative obligations.

        Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on consent, the need to provide a requested service or perform an agreement, compliance with a legal obligation or another lawful basis available under applicable law.

        BioWild may use browser storage or similar technologies where reasonably necessary for requested functions, interface preferences, security and website operation. The BioWild Shop may store cart information locally in the user's browser.

        Optional analytics, personalization or marketing technologies, if introduced, should be described clearly and used consistently with any consent requirement that applies.

        BioWild may process information needed to coordinate ticket requests, visits, merchandise orders, pickup or delivery, customer support, sponsorships and membership benefits.

        If a third-party payment provider is connected, that provider may collect payment credentials directly under its own privacy and security terms. Users should not assume BioWild itself stores full card numbers or CVV data unless a secure integration expressly states otherwise.

        Sponsorships are symbolic. Personal information used for recognition, certificates or sponsorship communications does not create ownership or custody rights over a bird.

        Birdify may process photos, audio, characteristics or other information submitted for identification and educational use. Uploaded media can contain people, voices, timestamps, device metadata or location information beyond the intended bird subject.

        Camera, microphone or location access should occur only through the appropriate browser or device permission mechanism when the user chooses a feature that requires it.

        If BioWild later uses uploaded media to train or materially evaluate machine-learning models beyond the service requested by the user, that additional use should first be disclosed and supported by an appropriate legal basis.

        A sighting may include species, date, time, media, comments and geographic coordinates. When linked to a person or account, some of that information may also constitute personal information.

        Exact wildlife locations can create conservation risks. BioWild may therefore generalize, delay, restrict or withhold precise locations for nests, threatened species, rehabilitation areas or other sensitive sites where appropriate.

        BioWild may disclose information when reasonably necessary to relevant hosting, email, reservation, payment, communications, security or technical providers; schools or conservation partners involved in an authorized program; professional advisers; or competent authorities where disclosure is legally required or permitted.

        BioWild does not intend to sell personal information as a commercial data product.

        Some cloud, email, map, communications or payment providers may store or process information outside Panama. When such a provider is used, BioWild should assess the arrangement under applicable Panamanian requirements and use appropriate safeguards where required.

        Retention depends on the purpose for which information was collected and any applicable legal, accounting, safety, conservation, contractual or dispute-resolution requirement.

        • General inquiries may be kept for a reasonable follow-up period.
        • Transaction records may require longer administrative or accounting retention.
        • Program records may remain during participation and for a reasonable period afterward.
        • Conservation observations may be retained for legitimate research or conservation purposes where lawful.

        Where information no longer needs to identify a person and no valid retention reason remains, BioWild should delete it or anonymize it where appropriate.

        BioWild should use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the type of information and the risks involved. Measures may include access controls, secure configuration, limited permissions, backups, staff practices and reputable service providers.

        No online or physical system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their own devices, accounts and communications.

        School visits, workshops, camps, volunteering or youth activities may require limited information about participation, emergency contacts, accessibility or safety. BioWild should collect only what is appropriate for the program.

        Where authorization from a parent, guardian, school or another responsible party is legally required, BioWild should obtain it. Public use of identifiable images or names of minors should be handled with additional care.

        Service communications may be sent when needed to operate a requested activity. Promotional communications should provide an appropriate way to stop receiving them where applicable.

        BioWild may link to social networks, maps, payment services, educational resources and other independent services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy notices and terms.

        Panama's data-protection framework recognizes rights including access, rectification, cancellation, opposition and portability, subject to the conditions and exceptions established by law.

        Privacy requests may be sent to biowild2026@outlook.com or through the BioWild contact page. BioWild may need proportionate information to verify the person making a request before disclosing or changing personal information.

        If you believe your data-protection rights have been violated, you may also have the right to raise the matter with Panama's competent data-protection authority, ANTAI, subject to its procedures and jurisdiction.

        BioWild may update this Policy when services, technology, providers, law or operations change. The effective date should be revised when a material update is published.

        Before commercial launch The final Policy should be reviewed against BioWild's actual legal entity, databases, forms, hosting, analytics, payment providers, retention practices and operating procedures.
