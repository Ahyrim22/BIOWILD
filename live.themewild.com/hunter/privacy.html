Privacy Policy

Understand what happens to your information.

This policy applies to BioWild's website and the personal information processed in connection with sanctuary visits, tickets, educational programs, volunteering, conservation activities, sponsorships, merchandise, communications, and digital conservation experiences.

Privacy By Design

Clear information before unnecessary collection.

BioWild aims to collect only personal information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service, use it for defined purposes, and provide people with meaningful information about how their data is handled.

01
Overview

Scope and Responsibility

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by BioWild in connection with its own website, sanctuary activities, conservation programs and related services in Panama.

This can include sanctuary visits, ticket requests, guided experiences, educational activities, volunteering, sponsorships, contact requests, newsletters, merchandise orders, wildlife observations and digital conservation features.

For personal information that BioWild determines the purposes and means of processing, BioWild acts as the party responsible for that processing under applicable law.

Our approach BioWild seeks to avoid collecting personal information simply because it may be technically possible to do so. Collection should be connected to a legitimate and identifiable purpose.

If a third-party provider independently determines how it processes information, its own privacy terms may also apply.

02
Data Categories

Information We May Collect

The information involved depends on how you interact with BioWild. Not every category below is collected from every visitor.

Contact Information Name, email address, phone number and information included in communications with BioWild.
Visit & Ticket Information Reservation information, visit date, party details and information required to coordinate sanctuary experiences.
Volunteer Information Interests, availability, relevant experience and other information voluntarily submitted for participation.
Shop & Order Information Products, quantities, prices, pickup or delivery choices and information needed to coordinate an order.
Communication Preferences Newsletter subscription preferences and communication choices you provide.
Technical Information Hosting or security systems may generate information such as IP address, browser, device, date, time and technical logs.
Conservation Observations Bird sightings, photographs, audio, observation dates or approximate locations where a feature allows you to submit them.
Program Information Information reasonably needed to coordinate school programs, workshops, conservation activities or other experiences.
Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information. Do not send health information, identity documents, financial credentials or other sensitive information through ordinary forms or email unless BioWild specifically requests it through an appropriate channel for a legitimate purpose.
03
Collection

How We Receive Information

Most information BioWild processes is provided directly by the person interacting with us.

  • Information you send by email or through a website form.
  • Information entered when requesting tickets, reservations or sanctuary activities.
  • Information submitted in a volunteer or educational-program request.
  • Merchandise selections saved in your browser or included in an order request.
  • Information submitted through conservation, bird-sighting or interactive features.
  • Technical information automatically generated by hosting, browser, security or infrastructure systems.
  • Information lawfully provided by a parent, guardian, school, organization, service provider or partner when relevant to an authorized activity.

If information is obtained from another lawful source and applicable law requires notification, BioWild will take reasonable steps to provide the required information.

04
Purpose

Why We Use Personal Information

BioWild uses personal information only for purposes reasonably connected with our services, conservation mission, administration, safety and legal responsibilities.

  • Respond to questions, bird-rescue reports, requests and messages.
  • Coordinate tickets, visits, tours, memberships, workshops and educational activities.
  • Review volunteer requests and organize volunteer participation.
  • Prepare and coordinate merchandise orders, pickup or delivery.
  • Manage sponsorship or conservation-support arrangements.
  • Send newsletters or promotional communications when you have chosen to receive them.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website and its features.
  • Support appropriate conservation research, wildlife monitoring and environmental education.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, security incidents or unauthorized activity.
  • Satisfy legal, accounting, administrative or contractual obligations.

Legal Basis

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on your consent, the need to provide a service or perform an agreement, compliance with a legal obligation, or another lawful basis available under applicable Panamanian law.

When processing depends on consent, you may withdraw that consent for future processing, subject to legal limitations and processing already lawfully completed.

Cookies and Optional Technologies

Functional browser technologies may be used where needed for website operation. If BioWild later introduces optional analytics, advertising or other technologies that require consent, appropriate information and choices should be provided before they are activated where required by law.

You may delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Removing local storage can also remove products saved in your BioWild shopping cart.

06
Transactions

Tickets, Shop and Payment Information

BioWild may process information needed to coordinate ticket requests, visits, merchandise orders, pickup, delivery and customer support.

The current frontend checkout may prepare an order request and open your email application rather than independently completing an electronic payment transaction.

Card information BioWild does not intentionally store a full payment-card number or CVV in the biowildCart browser storage.

If BioWild enables real online payments in the future, payment credentials should be handled through an authorized payment provider and subject to that provider's own privacy, security and transaction terms.

BioWild may retain appropriate transaction or order records where reasonably necessary for customer service, accounting, dispute handling, legal compliance or other legitimate administrative purposes.

07
Conservation Technology

Birdify and Interactive Features

Birdify is BioWild's interactive conservation concept intended to help people identify and learn about birds through information such as sound, habitat, diet, conservation status and sanctuary context.

Some Birdify capabilities described by BioWild may be planned, experimental or not yet available. This policy does not mean that every described technology is currently active.

Microphone and Audio

If a sound-identification feature becomes available, microphone access should occur only after the browser or device provides the appropriate permission mechanism and you choose to use that feature.

Audio submitted for identification may be processed for the purpose explained at the time of use. BioWild should not intentionally use the microphone for unrelated background recording.

Location

If a feature requires device location, BioWild will seek the relevant permission before accessing it and should use location information only for explained purposes such as sanctuary navigation, observations or species context.

You control device permissions. Microphone, camera and location permissions can normally be reviewed or revoked through your browser or device settings.
08
Disclosure

When Information May Be Shared

BioWild does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary for a legitimate purpose, such as to:

  • Website hosting, cybersecurity, cloud, email or technology service providers.
  • Payment processors if real online payment functionality is enabled.
  • Delivery services where an order requires delivery information.
  • Authorized contractors or professionals assisting BioWild with an appropriate service.
  • Research or conservation partners where appropriate safeguards and a legitimate purpose exist.
  • Government, regulatory, judicial or law-enforcement authorities where disclosure is legally required or otherwise lawfully permitted.
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting, security or compliance purposes.

Service providers should receive only information reasonably necessary for their role and should be subject to appropriate confidentiality, security or data-processing requirements where applicable.

09
Cross-Border Processing

International Data Processing

Some technology providers used by websites, email services, cloud infrastructure or future payment systems may operate servers or personnel outside Panama.

Where BioWild transfers or permits processing of personal information outside Panama, it should evaluate the transfer and implement appropriate legal, contractual, organizational and security safeguards as required by applicable law.

The use of an international provider does not remove BioWild's responsibility to consider the privacy requirements applicable to information under its control.

10
Storage Period

How Long We Keep Information

BioWild seeks to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or justified by law, accounting obligations, safety, dispute resolution or another legitimate requirement.

  • General inquiries may be retained while active and for a reasonable follow-up period.
  • Newsletter subscription information may be retained until you unsubscribe or the subscription is otherwise closed.
  • Order and transaction records may be retained for appropriate accounting, customer-service and legal periods.
  • Volunteer or program records may be retained during participation and for a reasonable administrative period afterward.
  • Browser cart information may remain on your own device until it is cleared by you, the website, checkout logic or browser settings.
  • Conservation observations may be retained for legitimate conservation or research purposes where appropriate and lawful.

Where possible and appropriate, information that no longer needs to identify a person may instead be deleted or anonymized.

11
Protection

Security of Personal Information

BioWild seeks to use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved.

Depending on the system, safeguards may include controlled access, appropriate account practices, secure hosting, maintenance, backups, data minimization, staff confidentiality and procedures intended to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No electronic system is absolutely secure. Users should also protect their devices, passwords and accounts and should avoid transmitting highly sensitive information through ordinary email.

If BioWild becomes aware of a personal-data security incident, it will evaluate the incident and take steps required by applicable law and appropriate security practice.

12
Young Participants

Children, Students and Educational Programs

Environmental education is an important part of BioWild's mission, and some activities may involve children and adolescents through school visits, workshops, camps or other educational experiences.

Where personal information about a minor is reasonably necessary, BioWild should limit collection to what is needed and obtain authorization from a parent, legal representative, school or other authorized responsible party where required.

BioWild should not intentionally request unnecessary sensitive information from minors through publicly accessible areas of the website.

A parent or legal representative who believes a minor's information has been improperly provided or processed may contact BioWild to request review and exercise any applicable rights.

Photos and recordings involving minors BioWild should obtain the appropriate authorization before using an identifiable minor's image for promotional purposes where consent or another legal basis is required.
13
Choice

Newsletters, Communications and Media

Newsletter

BioWild may send conservation stories, sanctuary news, events, volunteer opportunities or similar communications when you choose to receive them.

You may request to stop promotional communications at any time by using an unsubscribe mechanism when available or by contacting biowild2026@outlook.com.

The newsletter form currently used on some BioWild pages may open your email application rather than automatically registering an address in a dedicated subscription database.

Service Communications

Even if you do not wish to receive marketing, BioWild may still contact you where reasonably necessary regarding an active order, reservation, volunteer request, wildlife report, safety issue or other service you requested.

Photography and Media

If BioWild intentionally captures identifiable visitors for promotional or public communications, it should use an appropriate legal basis and obtain consent when required.

Immediate effect*

When applicable
Withdraw Consent

How to Exercise a Right

Send your request to biowild2026@outlook.com and clearly explain the right you wish to exercise and the information concerned.

BioWild may request reasonable information necessary to verify your identity before disclosing, correcting, deleting or transferring personal information. Verification should be proportionate and should not involve collecting unnecessary identification data.

Applicable law controls. The exact scope, conditions, exceptions and response periods for a request are determined by applicable Panamanian data-protection law.

Panama Data-Protection Framework

BioWild intends to process personal information consistently with applicable Panamanian data-protection requirements, including the general framework established by Law 81 of March 26, 2019 on Personal Data Protection and its applicable regulations.

Panama's National Authority for Transparency and Access to Information (ANTAI), through its Personal Data Protection Directorate, is the competent authority identified by the applicable framework for matters within its jurisdiction.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy limits a person's right to contact a competent authority or exercise any remedy available under applicable law.

Our Privacy Principles

Conservation technology should respect the people using it.

BioWild's approach to personal information is built around four straightforward ideas: know why data is needed, collect responsibly, protect it appropriately, and respect the person behind the information.

Principle 01

Purpose

Information should be collected for an identified purpose rather than simply because technology makes collection possible.

Principle 02

Minimization

BioWild should avoid requesting information that is not reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

Principle 03

Protection

Personal information should be protected through measures proportionate to its sensitivity and associated risks.

Principle 04

Individual Control

People should receive meaningful information and be able to exercise the privacy rights available to them.
