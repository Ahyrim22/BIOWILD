BioWild Privacy Policy

BioWild believes that protecting wildlife and respecting people should go hand in hand. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we may collect through our sanctuary, website, conservation programs, volunteer activities, Birdify features, ticketing services, newsletter, and BioWild Shop, and how we use and protect that information.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by BioWild through our website and services associated with our bird sanctuary and conservation activities in Panama.

This includes interactions involving sanctuary visits, ticket purchases, guided experiences, educational programs, volunteering, contact requests, newsletters, merchandise purchases, conservation activities, research-related digital features, and Birdify when those services are available.

For purposes of the personal information described in this policy, BioWild is responsible for determining why and how that information is processed in connection with its own services.

Our principle: BioWild seeks to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide our services, operate our sanctuary, protect users and wildlife, and fulfill our conservation mission.

2. Information We May Collect

The information BioWild collects depends on how you interact with us. You may provide some information directly, while certain technical information may be generated when you use our website.

Contact Information Name, email address, contact details, and information you include when contacting BioWild.
Visits & Tickets Information needed to manage reservations, tickets, visitor services, memberships, or guided experiences.
Volunteer Information Contact information, areas of interest, availability, relevant experience, and other details voluntarily provided when applying to participate.
Shop Information Products selected, quantities, prices, checkout information, delivery or pickup preferences, and order-related contact details.
Newsletter Preferences Email address and subscription preferences when you choose to receive BioWild news and conservation updates.
Technical Information Depending on our hosting and security systems, technical logs may include browser type, device information, IP address, dates, times, or website activity.
Information You Choose to Provide

If you communicate with BioWild by email, forms, events, volunteer applications, or other channels, we may process the information contained in your message in order to respond appropriately.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically necessary for a service and BioWild has requested it through an appropriate channel.

3. How We Use Information

BioWild may use personal information for legitimate sanctuary, conservation, educational, commercial, administrative, and safety purposes, including:

  • Responding to questions, requests, reports, and messages.
  • Managing ticket purchases, reservations, memberships, tours, school programs, workshops, and sanctuary experiences.
  • Processing volunteer applications and coordinating volunteer activities.
  • Managing BioWild Shop selections, orders, pickup or delivery arrangements, and checkout support.
  • Sending conservation news and newsletters when you choose to subscribe.
  • Operating, maintaining, protecting, and improving the BioWild website and its interactive features.
  • Supporting research, conservation, wildlife monitoring, environmental education, and sanctuary operations.
  • Preventing misuse, fraud, security incidents, or unauthorized activity.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory, accounting, administrative, or contractual requirements when applicable.
Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the situation, BioWild may process personal information based on your consent, because processing is necessary to provide a service or fulfill an agreement with you, because BioWild must comply with a legal obligation, or where another lawful basis recognized under applicable Panamanian law applies.

When processing depends on consent, you may withdraw that consent for future processing, subject to applicable legal requirements and any processing that was already lawfully completed.

4. BioWild Shop & Checkout

The BioWild Shop allows visitors to select sanctuary merchandise before continuing directly to checkout. To make this experience work without a separate cart page, product selections may be stored locally in your browser.

Shopping Cart Storage

The current BioWild Shop may use browser local storage under the identifier biowildCart to remember merchandise you select.

This information may include:

  • Product identifier.
  • Product name.
  • Product price.
  • Product image path.
  • Selected quantity.

This cart information is stored on your device so that your selected products can appear on the BioWild Checkout page.

Payment security: BioWild does not intend to store a complete payment card number or CVV in browser local storage. If real online payment processing is enabled, payment credentials should be handled securely by an authorized payment service provider and subject to that provider's own privacy and security practices.

Order contact information may be used to communicate about a purchase, coordinate pickup or delivery, provide support, maintain necessary transaction records, and satisfy applicable administrative or legal obligations.

5. Birdify & Interactive Technology

Birdify is BioWild's interactive conservation concept designed to help users identify birds through their songs and learn about species, habitat, diet, conservation status, and where birds may be found within the sanctuary.

If Birdify features that use a microphone or location become available, those functions may require permission from your device before they can access the relevant feature.

Bird Sound Recordings

When a visitor actively chooses to use a sound-identification feature, audio may be processed for the purpose of identifying a bird or improving the requested conservation experience. BioWild should not activate your microphone without the permission required by your browser or device.

Location Information

If a Birdify feature needs location information, BioWild will seek the relevant permission before accessing it. Location information should be used only for purposes explained to the visitor, such as helping determine relevant sanctuary information or nearby bird observations.

You can normally manage microphone, location, and similar permissions through your browser or device settings.

6. Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

BioWild may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage when needed to provide website functionality, remember user choices, maintain a shopping selection, improve performance, or protect our services.

Some technologies may be strictly functional, while future analytics or third-party services may involve additional tracking technologies. Where consent is legally required, BioWild should provide an appropriate choice before activating optional technologies.

Managing Browser Storage

You can remove cookies or local storage using your browser settings. Deleting local storage may remove merchandise saved in your BioWild shopping selection or reset other website preferences.

7. When Information May Be Shared

BioWild does not intend to sell personal information as part of its conservation or sanctuary activities.

Personal information may be shared only when reasonably necessary, for example with:

  • Technology, website hosting, cybersecurity, email, or cloud service providers supporting BioWild.
  • Payment processors when online payment services are enabled.
  • Delivery providers when a merchandise delivery service requires relevant contact or address information.
  • Authorized professionals, contractors, researchers, or conservation partners when a legitimate project requires appropriate information and suitable safeguards are in place.
  • Governmental, regulatory, judicial, law-enforcement, or other competent authorities when disclosure is legally required.

Service providers should receive only information reasonably necessary for the service they perform and should be expected to protect that information in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. International Data Processing

Some technology providers used by modern websites may operate servers or services outside Panama. If BioWild uses such providers and a transfer of personal information occurs, BioWild should evaluate the transfer and use appropriate legal, contractual, and security safeguards as required by applicable law.

9. How Long We Keep Information

BioWild seeks to keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or for a longer period where required by law, accounting requirements, dispute resolution, safety, or legitimate administrative needs.

The appropriate retention period may depend on the type of information and service involved.

  • General inquiries may be retained while the inquiry is active and for a reasonable follow-up period.
  • Newsletter information may generally be retained until the subscriber unsubscribes or the subscription becomes inactive.
  • Order and transaction records may need to be retained for reasonable accounting, customer-service, or legal purposes.
  • Volunteer records may be retained during participation and for a reasonable administrative period afterward.
  • Local shopping-cart information can remain on the user's device until it is removed by the website logic, checkout process, browser settings, or the user.

10. Security of Personal Information

BioWild seeks to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information being processed.

These measures may include access controls, secure account practices, restricted staff access, secure hosting, software maintenance, backups, and procedures designed to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

No website, electronic transmission, or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their own devices, accounts, browsers, and passwords and avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary email.

11. Children, Students & Educational Programs

Education is an important part of BioWild's conservation mission, and our activities may include school groups, environmental education, workshops, summer programs, or other experiences involving children and adolescents.

When personal information relating to a minor is necessary for a program, BioWild should collect only the information reasonably required and seek authorization from a parent, guardian, school, or other responsible adult when required by applicable law or the nature of the activity.

BioWild should not intentionally request unnecessary sensitive information from children through public areas of the website.

A parent or legal representative who believes that information relating to a minor has been provided improperly may contact BioWild to request review, correction, or deletion where appropriate.

12. Newsletters & Communications

BioWild may send newsletters, conservation stories, sanctuary updates, volunteer opportunities, event information, or similar communications when you choose to subscribe or otherwise consent to receive them.

You may request to stop receiving promotional communications at any time by using an unsubscribe mechanism when provided or by contacting us at: biowild2026@outlook.com .

Even after marketing communications are stopped, BioWild may still send non-promotional messages reasonably necessary for an order, reservation, volunteer activity, safety matter, or other active service.

13. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable Panamanian law and any legally recognized limitations, a person whose personal information is processed may have rights concerning that information.

1
Access

Request information about whether BioWild holds personal information about you and how it is being used.

2
Rectification

Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

3
Cancellation

Request deletion or cancellation of personal information where the applicable legal conditions are satisfied.

4
Opposition

Object to certain processing of personal information where the right applies.

5
Portability

Where applicable, request personal information in an appropriate structured, commonly usable format.

How to Submit a Request

To exercise a privacy right, contact BioWild at biowild2026@outlook.com . Please clearly describe your request and provide enough information for BioWild to reasonably identify the relevant records.

BioWild may need to verify your identity before disclosing, correcting, deleting, or transferring personal information in order to prevent unauthorized access.

14. Third-Party Websites & Services

The BioWild website may contain links to social media platforms, conservation organizations, payment services, educational resources, partner websites, or other third-party services.

BioWild is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of a third-party website or service. Visitors should review the privacy information provided by those services before submitting personal information to them.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BioWild may update this Privacy Policy when our sanctuary services, website, technology, legal obligations, or data practices change.

When material changes are made, we may update the date displayed at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the website or another suitable channel.

Visitors are encouraged to review this page periodically.

16. Contact BioWild About Privacy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise a data protection right, believe information has been handled incorrectly, or need additional information about BioWild's privacy practices, please contact us.
