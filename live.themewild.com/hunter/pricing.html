Support FAQ

Questions about sponsorship.

Open each question to understand how the program works before choosing a support level.

Ask BioWild
01 What does my sponsorship support? +
Your support contributes to BioWild's conservation mission, including areas such as wildlife care, habitat protection, environmental education, research, and sanctuary operations.
02 Does a sponsorship mean I own the bird? +
No. Sponsorship is symbolic and supports the bird and the sanctuary's conservation work. Wildlife remains under the care and responsibility of BioWild.
03 Can I give a sponsorship as a gift? +
Yes. A digital sponsorship certificate makes the program suitable as a meaningful gift for someone interested in birds, conservation, and wildlife.
04 Can I cancel my support? +
Support arrangements can be managed according to the payment system connected to BioWild. Contact the team if you need assistance.
05 Can I visit the sanctuary as a sponsor? +
The Harpy Eagle level includes an annual guided visit by appointment, subject to availability and sanctuary conditions.
