Our Story

About the BioWild Sanctuary.

BioWild brings wildlife rescue, conservation, environmental education, ecotourism, research, and technology into one experience. The sanctuary is designed to help people understand Panama's birds not as distant wildlife, but as an essential part of the country's ecosystems and natural identity.

Visitors can discover birds, learn why their habitats matter, explore conservation stories, participate in guided experiences, and connect with the sanctuary through digital tools such as Birdify.

“BioWild is Panama's living future.”

Our Purpose

Mission and Vision

BioWild is built around a simple idea: protecting birds requires action, education, collaboration, and long-term responsibility.


BIOWILD
Rescue · Learn · Protect · Inspire
Our mission

Protect, rehabilitate, relocate.

We seek to protect, rehabilitate and relocate birds in Panama through rescue, education and strategic alliances.

Our vision

Lead through innovation.

We aspire to lead bird conservation in Panama through innovative models, promoting environmental awareness and responsible ecotourism.

The BioWild Model

Four ideas working as one ecosystem.

The sanctuary is not built around a single activity. BioWild connects care, conservation, learning, and technology so every experience can contribute to a bigger purpose.

Wildlife Rescue

Supporting rescued and vulnerable birds through responsible care and rehabilitation.

Conservation

Protecting habitats and encouraging a deeper understanding of Panama's biodiversity.

Education

Turning each visit into a chance to learn, question, observe, and care about wildlife.

Technology

Using digital tools such as Birdify to make bird discovery more interactive and accessible.

How BioWild Works

From protection to participation.

BioWild is designed as a complete conservation experience where wildlife care and public engagement reinforce each other.
