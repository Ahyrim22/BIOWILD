Protecting birds means more than simply keeping them safe from immediate harm. It involves preserving their habitats, ensuring access to food and clean water, reducing threats caused by human activities, and creating environments where they can live, reproduce, and migrate safely.

Services

What We Offers To Panama

Discover our country as a safe and beautiful home for many bird species, with diverse habitats and rich biodiversity.

Bird Identification

BioWild provides an intelligent bird-identification tool that allows users to identify birds by uploading a photo, uploading an audio recording, or selecting visible characteristics.

Interactive Bird Guide

Users can explore a catalog of Panamanian bird species and use an interactive map to discover where different species can be found within the sanctuary.

Birdwatching & Guided Tours

BioWild offers guided birdwatching tours and nature experiences where visitors can learn about Panama's biodiversity while observing birds in their natural or protected environment.

Environmental Education

BioWild provides educational experiences through quizzes, challenges, badges, interactive species information, workshops, and conservation content.

Conservation Monitoring

Users can report bird sightings by recording the species, date, time, location, and optionally uploading a photograph to assist conservation efforts.

Reservations & Membership

BioWild offers visitors a digital platform to check visiting hours, purchase tickets, reserve guided tours, participate in special events, and access memberships.

Panama's Birds

Meet Our Birds

Discover some of Panama's most remarkable bird species and learn about their unique characteristics, habitats, and importance to our ecosystems.

The Harpy Eagle is Panama's national bird and one of the most powerful eagles in the world. It depends on large tropical forests and plays an important role as a top predator in the ecosystem. Habitat loss and human activity threaten its survival.

Harpy Eagle

Harpia harpyja

Famous for its colorful bill, the Keel-billed Toucan is one of the most recognizable birds of the tropical forests of Panama. It helps regenerate forests by dispersing seeds through the fruits it consumes.

Keel-billed Toucan

Ramphastos sulfuratus

The Blue Cotinga is known for the brilliant blue plumage of the male. It lives mainly in tropical forests and feeds primarily on fruit. Its presence is closely connected to healthy forest ecosystems.

Blue Cotinga

Cotinga nattererii

The Yellow-green Tyrannulet is one of Panama's nationally endemic birds, meaning it is found only in Panama. Protecting its habitat is especially important because species with such restricted ranges can be highly vulnerable to environmental changes.

Yellow-green Tyrannulet
