Panama's Birds

Meet Our Birds

Discover some of Panama's most remarkable bird species and learn about their unique characteristics, habitats, and importance to our ecosystems.

Harpia harpyja

Harpy Eagle

The Harpy Eagle is Panama's national bird and one of the most powerful eagles in the world. It depends on large tropical forests and plays an important role as a top predator in the ecosystem. Habitat loss and human activity threaten its survival.

Ramphastos sulfuratus

Keel-billed Toucan

Famous for its colorful bill, the Keel-billed Toucan is one of the most recognizable birds of the tropical forests of Panama. It helps regenerate forests by dispersing seeds through the fruits it consumes.

Cotinga nattererii

Blue Cotinga

The Blue Cotinga is known for the brilliant blue plumage of the male. It lives mainly in tropical forests and feeds primarily on fruit. Its presence is closely connected to healthy forest ecosystems.

Nationally Endemic Species

Yellow-green Tyrannulet

The Yellow-green Tyrannulet is one of Panama's nationally endemic birds, meaning it is found only in Panama. Protecting its habitat is especially important because species with such restricted ranges can be highly vulnerable to environmental changes.
