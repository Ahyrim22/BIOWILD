The People Behind BioWild

Seventeen people. One mission.

BioWild brings together people with different skills, perspectives and responsibilities around one shared purpose: protecting Panama's birds and creating meaningful conservation experiences for the community.

How We Work

Different skills. Shared responsibility.

BioWild is built around collaboration. Conservation works best when science, education, technology, creativity and community participation move in the same direction.

Focus 01

Conservation

Every project should contribute to the protection of birds, habitats and biodiversity.

Focus 02

Innovation

Technology and creativity can make conservation more accessible and engaging.

Focus 03

Education

Knowledge gives people the tools to understand why wildlife protection matters.

Focus 04

Teamwork

Seventeen people can achieve much more when everyone contributes toward the same goal.

Be Part Of The Change

The team is 17 people. The mission belongs to everyone.

You do not need to work at BioWild to help protect Panama's birds. Visit, volunteer, learn, support conservation or simply help more people understand why birds and their habitats matter.

Become A Volunteer Contact Our Team

We are dedicated to protecting Panama's birds and their natural habitats. Our sanctuary provides a safe environment for injured, rescued, and endangered birds while promoting conservation, education, and respect for wildlife.
