Bird Identification

Discover what is flying above you using photographs, sounds or visible characteristics.

BioWild's intelligent identification experience allows users to explore bird species using multiple forms of evidence. Instead of depending on a single visual characteristic, users can combine photographs, audio recordings and manually selected traits to improve identification and learn more about the species they encounter.

Audio Recognition
