Service 01

Bird Identification

Discover what is flying above you using photographs, sounds or visible characteristics.

BioWild's intelligent identification experience allows users to explore bird species using multiple forms of evidence. Instead of depending on a single visual characteristic, users can combine photographs, audio recordings and manually selected traits to improve identification and learn more about the species they encounter.

Photo Upload

Upload a bird photograph and explore possible species matches.

Audio Recognition

Use bird calls and recordings as another identification clue.

Visual Characteristics

Select visible traits such as size, colors or beak shape.

What the experience can provide

  • Species name
  • Scientific name
  • Habitat information
  • Diet and behavior
  • Distribution
  • Conservation status

Service 05

Bird Sightings & Conservation Monitoring

Turn individual observations into biodiversity data.

Users can record bird sightings by documenting species, date, time, location and optional photographs. Over time, these observations can form a valuable database for understanding species distribution and monitoring changes in bird populations.

Location

Record where the observation happened.

Observation Data

Store date and time for each sighting.

Evidence

Add photographs to support the observation.

Citizen science workflow

  • Observe
  • Identify
  • Record
  • Verify
  • Analyze
  • Conserve
