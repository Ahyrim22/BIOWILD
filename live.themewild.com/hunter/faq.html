Quick answers

Questions people ask most

Can I keep a wild bird?

Wild birds should not be treated as pets. Their welfare depends on appropriate habitat, behavior and care.

Can I photograph the birds?

Photography can be an excellent way to document biodiversity, provided it does not disturb the animals.

How can I help from home?

You can support conservation by protecting native habitats, reducing pollution and learning more about local biodiversity.

Still have a question?

Our team is here to help. Contact BioWild if you need information about visits, volunteering, wildlife rescue or conservation activities.
