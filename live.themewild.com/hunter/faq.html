Quick Answers

Three things visitors often ask.

Small questions can make a big difference in how people interact with wildlife. Responsible observation always comes first.

Wildlife Ethics

Can I keep a wild bird?

Wild birds should not be treated as pets. Their welfare depends on appropriate habitat, behavior, and care.

Responsible Observation

Can I photograph the birds?

Photography can be an excellent way to document biodiversity, provided it does not disturb the animals.

Everyday Conservation

How can I help from home?

You can support conservation by protecting native habitats, reducing pollution, and learning more about local biodiversity.

Understanding BioWild

Three principles behind every answer.

Whether the question is about visits, rescue, education, or volunteering, the same priorities guide BioWild's work.

01 · WELFARE

The bird comes first.

Human interaction should never take priority over the physical welfare and natural behavior of wildlife.

02 · KNOWLEDGE

Understanding creates respect.

Environmental education gives visitors the context needed to understand why birds and habitats matter.

03 · CONSERVATION

Protect the whole ecosystem.

Bird conservation also means protecting habitats, ecological relationships, and the systems wildlife depends on.
